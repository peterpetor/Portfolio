Privacy Policy for DropPost

Last updated: May 13, 2026

DropPost ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share your personal information when you install or use the DropPost Shopify Application (the "App") in connection with your Shopify-supported store.

1. Information We Collect

When you install the App, we are automatically able to access certain types of information from your Shopify account:

  • Shop Information: Your store domain, email address, language, and basic settings to provide core app functionality.
  • Order & Product Data: We access order details and product information strictly to generate upsell recommendations and track conversion statistics.
  • Customer Information: We may access basic customer data strictly for the purpose of identifying repeat purchases and triggering automated reorder emails if enabled.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the personal information we collect in order to provide and operate the App. Specifically, we use this information to:

  • Generate one-click upsell offers on the post-purchase page.
  • Provide analytics on your upsell performance and extra revenue generated.
  • Communicate with you regarding App updates, billing, or support requests.
  • Improve and optimize our App's performance and user experience.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or your customers' information to third parties. We only share your information in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We may share data with secure, trusted third-party services (like our hosting providers) strictly for the operation of the App.
  • Legal Compliance: We may share information to comply with applicable laws and regulations, to respond to a subpoena, search warrant, or other lawful request for information we receive, or to otherwise protect our rights.

4. Data Retention

When you place an order through your store, we maintain the necessary information to track conversions as long as you have the App installed. If you uninstall the App, we will securely delete your data within 48 hours upon receiving the mandatory Webhook request from Shopify, or upon your direct request.

5. Security

We take data security seriously. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time in order to reflect, for example, changes to our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any significant changes via email or within the App dashboard.
